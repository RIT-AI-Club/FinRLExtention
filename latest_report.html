01 · Company Overview
The Architecture of American Air Power
Lockheed Martin Corporation is the world's largest defense contractor, operating across four integrated business segments that span the full spectrum of modern warfare — from fifth-generation stealth fighters to hypersonic missile systems and space-based intelligence platforms.

The company's flagship program, the F-35 Lightning II, represents the most advanced multi-role combat aircraft in history and anchors a multi-decade revenue stream across allied nations. Alongside the F-35, Lockheed produces the F-16 Fighting Falcon, F-22 Raptor, C-130 Hercules, and C-5M Super Galaxy — a portfolio that defines the backbone of Western air superiority.

Beyond aviation, the Missiles and Fire Control segment delivers the PAC-3 Patriot interceptor and THAAD terminal defense system — systems in active deployment across conflict zones globally. The Rotary and Mission Systems segment encompasses Sikorsky helicopters and advanced radar systems, while Space Systems handles satellite constellations and strategic missile programs.
