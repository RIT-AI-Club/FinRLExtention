01 — Company Overview
Global Industrial
Conglomerate

Honeywell International Inc. operates as a global industrial conglomerate spanning four major verticals: aerospace technologies, building automation, industrial automation, and energy & sustainability solutions. The company serves commercial aviation, defense, oil & gas, chemicals, and smart building markets worldwide.

Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, Honeywell maintains a diversified portfolio of high-margin technology products and services, positioning itself at the intersection of industrial digitalization and energy transition — two of the most durable long-cycle investment themes of the decade.
