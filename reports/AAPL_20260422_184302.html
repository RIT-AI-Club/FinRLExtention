01 — Company Overview
The Walled Garden

Apple Inc. (AAPL) designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and online services. Flagship products include the iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and a rapidly expanding services ecosystem encompassing the App Store, Apple Music, and cloud services.


The company operates in the Technology Hardware and Consumer Electronics sector, commanding a market capitalization of $3.77 trillion as of Q4 CY2025. With over 2.2 billion active devices globally and an installed base generating recurring services revenue approaching a $100B annual run rate, Apple's ecosystem lock-in remains unparalleled.


Employee count stands at 166,000, generating an extraordinary $709,500 in profits per employee — a testament to Apple's operational leverage and premium positioning.
