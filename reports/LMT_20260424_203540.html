Lockheed Martin Corporation (NYSE: LMT) is a global security, aerospace, and defense company engaged in the research, design, development, manufacture, integration, and sustainment of advanced technology systems, products, and services. Formed in 1995 from the merger of Lockheed and Martin Marietta, the company is headquartered in Bethesda, Maryland, and operates across four primary business segments serving U.S. forces and allied nations worldwide. Revenue per employee stands at $610,146, reflecting the high-value, technology-intensive nature of its operations.
