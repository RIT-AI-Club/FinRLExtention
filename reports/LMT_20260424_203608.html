01  /  Company Overview
GLOBAL SECURITY
& AEROSPACE

Lockheed Martin Corporation (NYSE: LMT) is the world's largest defense contractor, operating across four primary business segments serving U.S. government agencies, international allies, and commercial customers. With a global footprint spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, LMT designs, develops, manufactures, integrates, and sustains advanced technology systems for air, land, sea, and space domains.

The company's entrenched position as prime contractor for the F-35 Lightning II — the most advanced multi-role fighter in history — anchors its Aeronautics segment, which accounts for approximately 50% of total sales. Long-term government contracts and a $194 billion backlog provide exceptional revenue visibility across multi-year program cycles.

  • Aeronautics F-35 Lightning II, F-22 Raptor, C-130 Hercules transports — ~50% of revenue
  • Missiles & Fire Control PAC-3 missiles, THAAD, Precision Strike Missile — $12.68B (17.9% of 2024 sales)
  • Rotary & Mission Systems Naval systems, radar, sensors, Sikorsky helicopters
  • Space Systems Satellites, strategic missiles, NASA services, Space Development Agency awards
