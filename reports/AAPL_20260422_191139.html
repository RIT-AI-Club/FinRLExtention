Section 01
Company Overview

Apple Inc. is a multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California, operating in the consumer technology, software, and digital services sectors.

The company designs, manufactures, and markets a comprehensive portfolio including flagship hardware products — the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods — alongside a rapidly growing Services business encompassing the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.

Apple's competitive moat stems from its vertically integrated ecosystem combining proprietary hardware, operating systems (iOS, macOS, watchOS, tvOS, and visionOS), and custom-designed Apple silicon chips that deliver a tightly controlled user experience.

The company serves individual consumers, businesses, educational institutions, and government customers globally.
