Macro Headwinds — Tariffs on China supply chains, persistent high interest rates, and consumer spending sensitivity tied to employment conditions.
Mac Mini Supply Constraints — Marked-up Mac minis flooding eBay amid shortages signal potential demand frustration and resale inflation (April 2026).

Emerging Market Penetration — Modest but growing presence in underpenetrated markets supports long-term installed base expansion beyond 2.2 billion devices.
